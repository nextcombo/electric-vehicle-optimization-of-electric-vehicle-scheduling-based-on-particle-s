# Electric Vehicle | Optimization of Electric Vehicle Scheduling Based on Particle Swarm Optimization (Matlab Code Implementation)

The development of our society has led to an increasing number of people choosing public transportation as their preferred mode of travel due to its environmental friendliness. Electric buses, powered by batteries, are seen as the appropriate technology for the electrification of road public transport. However, the transition from traditional diesel buses to electric ones faces significant challenges due to limitations in range and charging times required for battery-powered buses.

Therefore, it is necessary to study how to optimize the scheduling of battery electric buses. In my research, the optimization targets include minimizing the total number of buses operated within a day, reducing charging costs, and minimizing battery costs.

This study involves maintaining the health of batteries, optimizing the dispatching and charging processes of electric buses. In the paper, I selected two bus routes operated by Shanghai No. 3 Bus Company for research. Based on my target model and particle swarm optimization algorithm, I achieved optimized results within a day, reducing the use and charging time of buses. Finally, I created a scheduling diagram for battery electric buses.
